This website has no patient forms, accounts, analytics or tracking cookies. Do not send personal or medical information. Request links lead to a local demonstration page, not to a practice or consultation supplier.

The hosting provider may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Links to the NHS website, NHS 111 online and the NHS App open external websites with their own privacy information. This demonstration is not a real practice privacy notice and does not describe any clinical record processing.
