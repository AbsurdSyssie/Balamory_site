# Hazard log (starter)

This is a starter hazard log for the Open GP Practice Website template. It records the hazards the template is designed to control and how. The risk ratings below had an initial review by Marcus Baw in October 2026.

A practice publishing a site made with this template is responsible for the accuracy of its own content. Practices and ICBs carrying out a DCB0160 assessment can use this log as a starting point.

Ratings use the DCB0129 risk matrix: severity (1 minor to 5 catastrophic) and likelihood (1 very low to 5 very high).

## Open actions

### Fictional Isle of Mull demonstration (1 November 2026)

H9: the owner clarified that this is an English GP service-design demo with a whimsical fictional Mull setting. `nation: england` now selects NHS 111 online, nhs.uk health information and the NHS App information link without a geography caveat. Scotland-only scaffolding is removed, not extended into production support. There is no NHS endorsement claim.

H2/H7: no ODS code, postcode, map, CQC identity/rating or earnings figures are fabricated. Missing regulatory/identity facts are allowed only by the narrow inert-demo predicate: literal `demo: true` and exact `/online-requests/` consultation URL. That predicate also selects the fictional presentation; the literal boolean always enables the global banner and noindex. A supplied CQC identity/widget is rejected for the fictional demo; supplied ODS and earnings still undergo validation, as do all embeds. Real England and Wales requirements remain unchanged. Negative tests cover false, absent, string and numeric demo values and unsafe supplied controls. The preview publishing gate also requires England. Switching off demo cannot produce a valid practice from these details.

H4/H10: all six homepage request actions and appointment, prescription, registration, results, fit-note, admin, complaints and telephone journeys stay on the exact local `/online-requests/` placeholder. No supplier embeds, forms or trackers. The fictional Mull address, staff, Ofcom phone, photo and attribution remain unchanged. Frozen-fixture coverage in `tests/build.test.js` and `tests/fictional-demo.test.js` does not depend on editable practice details.

H1: the dedicated urgent-help page retains its NHS.UK warning callout, h2 and exact existing life-threatening-emergency/999 wording. All pages still contain 999 and 111 telephone links plus English online urgent signposting. Build tests and the deployment audit check these controls, absence of Scottish destinations and absence of fictional CQC ratings. The existing clinical wording is not newly authored advice.

- Re-review ratings when the template changes in a way that affects a hazard or control.
- Add automated accessibility testing (for example axe) to CI.
- Consider a Content Security Policy generated from the trusted embed domains.
